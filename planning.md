Topic | Questions | Answers |
------|-----------|---------|
Purpose of Website | What is the purpose and goal of the website?| The purpose of this website is to highlight different club activities with the goal of gaining the attention of students and bringing in new members to the clubs |
Target Audience | Describe the target audience (age, gender, demographics) | The audience will consist of students attending the school. The idea is to bring in as many willing and open minded members as possible, regardless of age, gender, or ethnic background, so long as they have an interest and drive to participate in club activities. We will also try to gain support from teachers at the school and parents of club members for future funding needs and chaperones for remote activities outside of school.
Graphics | What graphics will you use on the website? | Graphics will consist of a wide variety of photos and videos of club activities from prior years, as well as a showcase of potential activities for the upcoming year. This way, students will feel inclined to join in on any events that grab their attention.
Color | What colors will you use within the site to enhance the purpose and brand? | The color palette will consist of the school's colors placed on top of a warm, neutral background to make increase ease of visibility and stick to the school theme.
Accessibility | How will the website accommodate people with disabilities? | The website will include text descriptions for all photos and videos, audio recordings, and text-to-speech options, and scalable font size options.
Project Timeline | Identify the project timeline | A 4 week time span structure to ensure efficient web development completed in time before club registration, with an extra week for final touches and reworks before launch. Week 1: Wireframe, Week 2: Coding, Week 3: Testing, Week 4: Final Touches and Launch
